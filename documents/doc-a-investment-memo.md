## 1. Executive Summary

The Investment Team recommends that the Committee approve the acquisition of
the Wrenfield Residential Solar Portfolio. The portfolio comprises 250 MW of
aggregate installed and contracted capacity across three states, with a
committed expansion program scheduled through 2027.

The expansion program installation cost is estimated at $186M, funded from a
combination of committed debt facilities and sponsor equity. The expansion
tranche targets commercial operation in Q4 2027.

We believe the portfolio's contracted cash flows, established installation
partner relationship, and demonstrated operating history support the proposed
valuation.

## 2. Transaction Overview

Wrenfield's asset base consists of distributed residential solar systems under
long-term customer agreements, together with a contracted expansion pipeline.
Aggregate capacity across the installed base and contracted expansion is
250 MW. The transaction assumes completion of the expansion program on the
current schedule, with the expansion tranche reaching commercial operation in
Q4 2027.

## 3. Key Counterparties

Installation and expansion works are performed under a master installation
agreement with Freedom Forever LLC, executed January 2026, which remains in
good standing. Freedom Forever is one of the largest residential solar
installers in the United States, providing national coverage and established
installation crews across the portfolio's target markets.

The expansion is underwritten on the federal investment tax credit at its
current rate, which requires that qualifying property be placed in service by
December 31, 2027. The schedule carries roughly nine months of headroom
against that date.

The installer-backed 25-year workmanship warranty covers the installed base,
which we view as a meaningful mitigant to long-term operations and
maintenance risk.

## 5. Risks and Mitigants

- **Construction cost inflation.** The installation cost estimate reflects
  executed pricing under the master installation agreement; escalation
  exposure is limited to uncontracted scope.
- **Counterparty concentration.** Installation works are concentrated with a
  single partner; this is mitigated by the partner's national scale and the
  warranty structure described above.
- **Schedule risk.** The Q4 2027 commercial operation target includes buffer
  against permitting and interconnection timelines observed to date.

## 6. Recommendation

The Investment Team recommends approval of the transaction as presented,
subject to confirmatory diligence and final documentation.
