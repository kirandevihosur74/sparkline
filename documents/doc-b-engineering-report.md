# Independent Engineering Report

**Subject:** Technical Review — Wrenfield Residential Solar Portfolio
**Date:** February 10, 2026
**Prepared for:** Halcyon Infrastructure Partners

## 1. Scope of Review

Ardenfell Engineering Advisors was engaged to perform an independent technical
review of the Wrenfield Residential Solar Portfolio in connection with a
proposed acquisition. Our review covered the installed asset base, the
contracted expansion program, cost assumptions, and the delivery schedule.
This report presents our findings and the basis for our conclusions.

## 2. Technical Review

Aggregate portfolio capacity of 250 MW was verified against interconnection
documentation provided in the data room. The installed base shows performance
consistent with regional irradiance expectations, and availability figures are
within industry norms for distributed residential assets.

The expansion program design assumes Tier-1 440 W modules, which we consider
appropriate for residential applications and consistent with current
procurement availability.

## 3. Cost Assessment

We estimate total expansion installation cost at $211M. Our estimate reflects
current labor rates in the portfolio's target markets, observed equipment
pricing, and mobilization costs across a distributed installation program of
this scale. We note that our estimate is not reconciled to the sponsor's
internal figures and should be read as an independent bottom-up assessment.

The O&M cost assumption of $14.2M per year is within market range for a
portfolio of this composition and geographic spread.

## 4. Schedule

Commercial operation of the expansion tranche is achievable by Q4 2027,
provided installation mobilization proceeds on the currently contemplated
timeline and permitting activity continues at observed rates.

## 5. Summary of Findings

## 6. Assumptions and Limitations

This review relies on documentation provided in the data room as of the report
date. We have not independently verified counterparty contractual standing or
financing arrangements, which fall outside the scope of this engagement.
